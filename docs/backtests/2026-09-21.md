# Parameter search — does faster rotation improve risk/return? (2026-09-21)

**Question (user, 18 Sep):** find the most optimal trading trend algorithmically, with a
shorter interval, and push the risk/return ratio.

**Verdict: no change to the live rules.** Faster rotation does not pay after DR costs,
"optimal" parameters do not survive out-of-sample, and the ฿150k floor already binds at
today's risk level.

## Setup
- 14 DR-eligible US underlyings in THB, live structure: momentum sleeve 55% + TDEX 25% + 20% cash.
- Engine: `src/backtest/engine.py` (decide on close, fill at next open). BLS fee 0.2215%/side.
- Spread cost: 35 bps per side = half of a one-tick spread, averaged over the 14 DRs
  (range 20 bps MSFT01/NVDA06 to 47 bps AMZN06/PLTR01/SPOT06). Sensitivity at 60 bps.
- Picks unchanged at a rebalance date = no trades (mirrors the live dust filter).
- Grid: lookback 21/42/63/126/252 d x rebalance every 5/10/21/63 trading days x top 2/3/5
  x SMA filter none/100/200 = 180 runs. Scored 2022-08-17 -> 2026-09-18.
- Walk-forward: in-sample to 2024-08-30, out-of-sample (OOS) after. Full table: `2026-09-21-grid.csv`.

## 2. Can parameters be "optimised"? No.
Rank correlation between in-sample and out-of-sample results across the 180 configs:
**Calmar 0.08, Sharpe 0.03.** Knowing which settings were best in 2022-24 told you nothing
about which were best in 2024-26. The hindsight OOS winner (42 d lookback, quarterly, top-2)
was in the bottom fifth in-sample. Picking the best backtest is fitting noise.

The only stable dial is concentration, and it is a risk dial, not skill:
| top-N | OOS CAGR | OOS maxDD |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | 31.2% | 16.5% |
| 3 | 49.4% | 20.6% |
| 2 | 63.5% | 21.9% (worst phase 29.9%) |

## 3. The floor already binds
At 80% deployed, worst drawdowns were 21-25% for top-3. From ฿203k that is ฿152-160k:
the ฿160k de-risk line and the ฿155k liquidation line. Top-2's worst case (29.9%) is ฿142k,
through the ฿150k floor. There is no room to add risk without breaking the floor rule.

## Caveats
- Universe is hindsight-picked (names that earned a Thai DR), so absolute CAGRs are inflated;
  only the comparisons between settings mean anything.
- Signals and fills use the US underlying in THB, not the DR's own quotes; DR premium/discount
  and thin overnight liquidity are not modelled (they would hurt fast rotation more).
- End-of-day data only; nothing here speaks to intraday trading.
- Spike code was throwaway (not in the repo); the engine and strategy classes it used are.
